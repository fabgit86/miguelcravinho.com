Hi

I'm Miguel, a sound designer based between London and Portugal, and I spend an unhealthy amount of time listening to things most people ignore.

I turn ordinary recordings, from rusty gates and old machinery to smashed vegetables and the perfect wind, into sounds that make games, films and animation feel real. If you care about the little details, get in touch.
